World-class

LeisureWorld Swim School runs year-round at all three of our Cork centres. Our qualified instructors follow a proven progression pathway, ensuring every swimmer — young or old — builds confidence, technique, and safety awareness at their own pace.

Whether your child is stepping into the water for the very first time or working towards a lifeguard qualification, we have a programme built exactly for where they are right now.

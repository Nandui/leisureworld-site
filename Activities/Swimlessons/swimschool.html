Before the
first splash.

A few things to know before your child joins us in the water.

Ask our swim team
Does my child need to know how to swim?

No. Water Safety & Fun welcomes beginners aged five and over. Starfish introduces water confidence, and children progress at their own pace.

What should we bring to the assessment?

Bring swimwear, a swim cap and a towel. Goggles are optional for the assessment and required for lessons. A parent or guardian must stay on-site.

How does my child move up a level?

Each level has skills to work towards. Instructors move swimmers on when they can confidently and consistently meet those competencies.

Parent account guide
Where can I check prices and class times?

Course search lets you choose a centre and swimming category. Check the dates and price on the course details before booking. Reception can help if the course you need is not listed.

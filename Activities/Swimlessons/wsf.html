Water safety
& fun.

Little splashes. Big first steps. A beginner programme for children building confidence in the water.

Book a free assessment Compare programmes
Who it’s for
Beginners aged 5+
Where
Bishopstown
Progression
Four levels

What your child will work on

Four levels. A little more confidence.

From Starfish to Dolphins, each level builds on the last. Your instructor helps your child progress when they are ready.

01

Starfish

Build confidence with putting the face in the water, floating and gliding.

02

Penguins

Get comfortable in the larger pool and work on kicking, swimming on the back and retrieving objects.

03

Turtles

Develop full strokes, treading water and supervised deep-water skills.

04

Dolphins

Work on breathing, longer distances and confidence in deeper water.

What should we bring?

Bring swimwear, a swimming hat and a towel. Ask the team about any extra items needed for your child’s group.

Does a parent or guardian need to stay?

A parent or guardian must stay on site throughout a child’s swimming lesson.

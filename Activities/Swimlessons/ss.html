What is Swimming
Skills?

Water Safety and Fun is a beginner-level swimming course, ideal for children aged 5 and above who have little to no prior experience in the water.

Kids will learn how to be safe in shallow and deep water, perform the main swim strokes — front crawl, back crawl and breaststroke — and enjoy a huge range of fun water activities along the way.

The course is divided into 4 levels: Starfish, Penguins, Turtles and Dolphins. Each contains competencies that, when achieved, allow the child to progress to the next level. Every child develops at their own pace, and we only move kids on when the competencies are fully met.

The Levels

Main goals
of each level

Each level has a set of competencies that, when achieved, allow the child to move up. Children progress only when they're truly ready.
01
⭐
Shark 1
Level One
Goal

Helping children feel comfortable and confident in the pool environment. Kids build trust in their own abilities and take their very first steps with water.

Competencies
Put face in the water
Float on front
Float on back
Glide confidently through the water
02
🐧
Shark 2
Level Two
Goal

Adapting to the larger pool and building strength and coordination. Kids extend their water skills and work towards more independent swimming.

Competencies
Get used to the big pool
Kick legs for longer distances
Swim with belly up to the sky
Retrieve objects from pool floor
Swim through a hoop
03
🐢
Swim Club
Level Three
Goal

Introducing more advanced swimming techniques and deep-water activities. Kids begin to refine their strokes and develop essential survival skills.

Competencies
Use arms for all strokes
Tread water
Jump & surface dive in deep end
Full front & back crawl stroke
Introduction to breaststroke

Each swimmer progresses at their own pace. We only move swimmers to the next level once all competencies are fully met. There is no rush — every skill earned is a genuine achievement worth celebrating.

🏆 After Swim Club — What's next?

Completing Swim Club marks the end of the Swimming Skills programme. Swimmers who want to take their skills further are welcome to explore our competitive swimming pathway — speak to a member of our team at reception for details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Water Safety and Fun is designed for children aged 5 and above. Your child does not need any prior swimming experience — the Starfish level is specifically built for complete beginners who are just getting comfortable in the water.
Each level has a defined set of competencies. Your child's instructor will assess their progress and only move them up once every skill has been confidently achieved. We respect each child's personal development — there is no fixed timeline, only progress done right.
After graduating from Dolphins, your child will be a confident, safe swimmer. If they're passionate about swimming and want to continue — whether joining a swim club or swimming competitively in the future — we also offer an advanced course called Swimming Skills. Ask at reception for details.
You can sign up online using the Join Online button above, or simply call in to LeisureWorld Bishopstown and speak to a member of our team. We'll assess your child's current level and place them in the right group to get started.

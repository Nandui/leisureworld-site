What your child will work on

Put confidence to good use.

A chance for confident swimmers aged 8–14 to explore water safety, lifesaving and first aid skills under an instructor’s supervision.

01

Water safety

Learn to recognise situations and practise lifesaving swimming skills.

02

Rescue awareness

Explore supervised rescue skills and ways to assist different casualties.

03

First aid skills

The programme introduces first aid, CPR and AED awareness with an instructor.

04

Progress with confidence

Your instructor assesses readiness and progression. Ask reception about the current groups and award structure.

What should we bring?

Bring swimwear, a swimming hat and a towel. Ask the team about any extra items needed for your child’s group.

Does a parent or guardian need to stay?

A parent or guardian must stay on site throughout a child’s swimming lesson.

What is Rookie
Lifeguard?

The Rookie Lifeguard programme is an exciting, award-winning course designed to introduce young swimmers to the world of lifeguarding. Building on strong swimming foundations, participants develop essential water safety, rescue, and survival skills.

Participants learn real lifeguard techniques in a fun and structured environment — from spotting hazards to performing water rescues — all tailored to their age and ability level.

The programme is divided into three award levels: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. Each level builds progressively on the last, challenging participants to develop greater skill, confidence, and responsibility in and around the water.

🏅 After Gold — What's next?

Achieving the Gold Rookie Lifeguard award is a fantastic accomplishment. Graduates who wish to pursue lifeguarding further may be eligible to progress towards the National Pool Lifeguard Qualification (NPLQ). Ask at reception to find out more about our lifeguard training pathways.

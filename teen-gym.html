Teen gym

Build confidence in the gym, with a proper introduction and support from qualified instructors.

Start with an induction

Teen gym is available at Bishopstown and Churchfield. Before using the gym or attending fitness classes, every teen must complete a safety induction with a member of the gym team. A parent or legal guardian must attend the induction.

Contact reception to arrange the induction and check which sessions are suitable for your age. The admission policy distinguishes teen sessions for ages 13–15 and student sessions from age 16.

Arrange a teen induction

Teen Fitness Academy

Learn the basics of exercise, warm-ups, cool-downs and bodyweight movements, then progress with an instructor as your confidence grows.

Bishopstown

Saturday sessions last 45 minutes: girls at 2pm and boys at 3pm. Check current availability before attending.

Churchfield

Academy sessions are not yet listed. Ask reception to register your interest.

See the Bishopstown class timetable

Prices and booking

A teen pay-as-you-go visit is €9. Discounted membership options are also available. Ask the team about pool access after your gym session; swimming is subject to the public timetable and supervision requirements.

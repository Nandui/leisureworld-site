Gus Healy Pool

A well-loved 25-metre pool in Douglas Village, open for public lane and leisure swimming, and structured swimming lessons for all ages.

Book a Session View Pricing

Douglas's community pool

The Gus Healy Pool is a well-loved facility in the heart of Douglas Village, named in honour of Gus Healy, a former Lord Mayor of Cork who championed public leisure facilities for the city.

The pool is open to the public on a pay-as-you-go basis — no membership needed. Simply turn up, pay at reception, and swim.

Swimming lessons for all ages and abilities are available throughout the year. Contact the centre directly for current lesson schedules.

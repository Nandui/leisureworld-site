At home on the northside

More ways
to feel good.

A swim, a workout, or a splash with the family. Make a little time for yourself at LeisureWorld Churchfield, on Cork’s northside.

With three pools, gym spaces, fitness classes and all-weather pitches, there’s room to find your own way to move.

View prices

  • Three pools

    A 25m pool, learner and toddler pools for swimming and play.

  • Waterslide & play

    A waterslide and children’s pool with a play zone.

  • Gym & fitness

    Cardio, resistance and free weights, plus fitness studios.

  • Teen gym

    Supervised training sessions for ages 13–17.

  • Sauna & steam

    A little time to unwind. For adults aged 18 and over.

  • Astro pitches

    Eight floodlit, all-weather pitches. Book ahead for your game.

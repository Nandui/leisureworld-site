The northside's community centre

LeisureWorld Churchfield is a beloved community facility on Cork's northside, serving the local community with top-quality leisure facilities at affordable prices.

Whether you're bringing the kids for a splash, training for a sport, or just looking to stay active, Churchfield has something for everyone.

Membership at Churchfield includes full access to LeisureWorld Bishopstown too — two centres for the price of one.

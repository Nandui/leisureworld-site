ABOUT
Who We Are

Cork's community
leisure network

Making health, fitness, and recreation accessible to everyone in Cork since 1997.

LeisureWorld Cork is a community-focused leisure network, operated by LW Management on behalf of Cork City Council.

Our mission is simple: make health, fitness, and recreation accessible to everyone.

Since opening our first centre in 1997, we've grown across Cork, bringing high-quality facilities into local communities with inclusive pricing and a genuinely welcoming atmosphere.

At the heart of everything we do is our ethos: Every Body Belongs.

Our Ethos

Every Body Belongs.

We believe that leisure, fitness, and wellbeing should be available to everyone — regardless of age, ability, background, or budget. That belief shapes every decision we make.

From our concession pricing to our adapted swimming sessions, from our teen gym programme to our older adult fitness classes — everything we do is built around real people and real Cork lives.

We're not a commercial gym. We're a community facility. There's a difference, and we're proud of it.

Governance

How we run

LeisureWorld Cork is operated by LW Management CLG, a not-for-profit company limited by guarantee, on behalf of Cork City Council.

As a community leisure operator, any surplus generated is reinvested directly into our facilities and programmes — ensuring that LeisureWorld continues to serve the people of Cork for generations to come.

We work in close partnership with Cork City Council, the HSE, Swim Ireland, Cork Sports Partnership, and a range of local community organisations to deliver the broadest possible range of activities and programmes.

LW Management CLG

Not-for-profit operator of all three LeisureWorld Cork centres. Any surplus is reinvested into facilities and community programmes.

Cork City Council

Owner of the LeisureWorld Cork facilities. LeisureWorld is operated on the Council's behalf to serve the wider Cork community.

Community Partners

We work with the HSE, Swim Ireland, Cork Sports Partnership, Down Syndrome Cork, Headway, Neptune Basketball, and many more.

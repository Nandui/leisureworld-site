Using this website

Website
accessibility.

Our aim is to make LeisureWorld information and booking routes usable by as many people as possible.

Accessibility status.

This statement applies to the public pages in this LeisureWorld website redesign. The implementation target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA, alongside the relevant web requirements of EN 301 549.

A full accessibility conformance evaluation has not yet been completed. This website therefore does not make a claim of full compliance. Automated checks and selected keyboard, layout and interaction checks are part of the redesign review.

Statement prepared on 12 September 2026.

Ways to use the website.

  • Use the “Skip to content” link to bypass navigation.
  • Use a keyboard to follow links, open the menu, filter classes and read expandable answers.
  • Increase browser zoom or use a narrow screen; page content is designed to reflow.
  • Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
  • Use readable HTML versions of centre policies instead of relying on downloads.

Known limitations and further checks.

  • The external Legend booking and membership service, app stores and other linked services have not been fully assessed as part of this website work.
  • Some linked documents and third-party pages may use a different format or may not be fully accessible.
  • Comprehensive testing with screen readers, speech input, disabled users and the deployed booking journey is still required.

If a booking route or document prevents you from completing a task, contact reception for assistance. Giving feedback does not require you to use the enquiry form.

Feedback and assistance.

Email info@leisureworldcork.com or call 021 434 6505. Tell us which page or task caused difficulty and what information or assistance you need. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis.

If you are dissatisfied with the response, you can ask about the complaints process. Information about escalating public-service accessibility complaints is available from the Office of the Ombudsman.

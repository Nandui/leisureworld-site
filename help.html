You can join online in a few minutes through our online sign-up, or simply call into any of our three centres and our reception team will set you up. No appointment needed — just bring photo ID and your payment details.
We offer monthly direct-debit memberships with full access to the pool, gym and fitness classes, as well as pay-as-you-go visits for those who prefer to drop in. Student, family and concession rates are available. See our pricing page for the latest rates across all centres.
Yes. Memberships can be frozen for medical reasons, travel, or other circumstances; just let your home centre know. Cancellation notice periods apply and are outlined in our terms and conditions. Pop into reception or contact us and we'll talk you through it.
All payments are handled through our secure, encrypted booking and direct-debit system. We never store full card details on-site, and your information is processed in line with GDPR and our privacy policy.
For most public and leisure swim sessions you can simply walk in on a pay-as-you-go basis. During busy periods or for specific lane sessions we'd recommend checking the timetable in our app or calling ahead, as pool space can fill up.
We run structured junior and adult lessons across our centres, grouped by ability level. Lessons run in terms, and places are allocated on enrolment. You can find current availability and enrol on our swimming lessons page or by contacting your nearest centre directly.
Bishopstown has an 18m pool, a 25m pool and a kids' pool. Churchfield has a 25m pool, a kids' pool and a poolside waterslide. The Gus Healy Pool in Douglas is a 25m competitive pool used for public and club swimming. See our centres page for full facility lists.
Bishopstown and Churchfield are typically open 7:00am–8:45pm on weekdays and 9:00am–5:45pm at weekends. The Gus Healy Pool in Douglas has its own pool-only schedule. Hours can change on bank holidays, so always check our app or opening hours section for the latest times.
All three centres offer on-site parking and step-free access to main facilities. If you have specific accessibility needs, please get in touch ahead of your visit and we'll make sure everything is ready for you. More detail is on our accessibility page.
Yes — gift vouchers are available to purchase online and can be redeemed against memberships, swims, lessons and more. Grab one through our gift voucher store.

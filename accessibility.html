The Functional Zone

A dedicated gym at LeisureWorld Bishopstown, designed with adapted equipment so people living with a neurological condition can train optimally and sustain an exercise programme — with the right support in place, every session.

About the Functional Zone

Adapted equipment, expert support

The Functional Zone is a specialist gym space at our Bishopstown centre, fitted with equipment adapted to suit people with a range of physical impairments. Everything about the space — the equipment, the pace, the guidance — is designed to make regular, meaningful exercise possible.

Every session is led by a chartered physiotherapist, with a qualified gym instructor also on the floor to supervise. That combination means you have both clinical expertise and hands-on exercise support, so you can train confidently and safely from your very first visit.

Led by a Physiotherapist A chartered physio designs and leads each session, tailored to your goals.
Supervised by an Instructor A qualified gym instructor is on the floor to support you throughout.

Who the Functional Zone is for

The space is designed for people whose physical function has been affected by a neurological condition — whether that was a single event, or an ongoing condition they manage over time.

People recovering from a single neurological event

If you've experienced a one-off neurological event that has left you with physical impairment, the Functional Zone offers a structured way to rebuild strength, movement and independence at your own pace.

Examples include
Stroke Acquired brain injury Spinal cord injury

People living with a progressive neurological condition

If you have a long-term progressive neurological condition, regular supervised exercise can support mobility, strength and quality of life. Our team will help you build a sustainable routine that works around how you're feeling.

Examples include
Parkinson's Multiple Sclerosis Motor neurone disease

How to get started

Access to the Functional Zone is currently through the HSE physiotherapy service. If you're not yet linked in, your GP can start the process for you.

1

Speak with your GP

Book a routine appointment with your GP and let them know you'd like to be referred for physiotherapy.

2

HSE Physiotherapy Referral

Your GP will refer you to the HSE physiotherapy service, which handles access to the Functional Zone.

3

Blackrock Hall

The team at Blackrock Hall will be in touch to assess suitability and next steps.

4

Start in the Zone

Once approved, you'll begin sessions in the Functional Zone — physio-led, instructor-supervised.

For more information

Contact Blackrock Hall

To find out more about getting started with the Functional Zone, or to check where you are in the referral process, get in touch with the team at Blackrock Hall directly.

Access is currently only via the HSE physiotherapy service. Blackrock Hall can guide you through what that looks like.

The Functional Zone.

Location
LeisureWorld Bishopstown
Access route
HSE Community Physiotherapy

Adapted space for exercise.

The Functional Zone is a gym area at Bishopstown with adapted equipment for people whose physical function is affected by a neurological condition.

How to get started.

Access to Functional Zone sessions is currently through the HSE Community Physiotherapy service. LeisureWorld does not decide who is referred. Speak to your healthcare team about that route.

For questions about the facility itself, contact LeisureWorld reception.

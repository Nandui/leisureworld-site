12 classes & programmes

Fitness

Revelate

A 45-minute cardio workout using rowers, bikes and ski machines, with music to keep you moving. Coached and uncoached sessions are available.

Fitness

Total Tone

A full-body class combining resistance training, bodyweight exercises and core work. Suitable for adults at different fitness levels.

Fitness

Legs, Bums & Tums

A 45-minute workout focused on your lower body and core, using weights and bodyweight movements.

Details and requirements

The published class is for women and welcomes beginners. Ask the team if you need help choosing a suitable class.

Fitness

Aquafit

A low-impact, music-based workout in the pool, using water resistance. You do not need to be able to swim.

AgeWell

AgeWell Movement

Movement, strength and balance exercises for older adults. You do not need a particular health condition to attend.

AgeWell

AgeWell Boxercise

Boxing-style movements to work on fitness, strength and coordination. No previous boxing experience is needed.

AgeWell

AgeWell Strength

A 45-minute class using dumbbells, barbells and floor exercises to build strength, balance and stability.

Details and requirements

This is a higher-intensity AgeWell class. The instructor adapts exercises to the individual; ask the team if you are unsure where to start.

AgeWell

AgeWell Stretch

Stretching, mobility and balance exercises, with time to focus on breathing. Suitable for different ages.

Teens · 13–17

Learn to Lift

Learn lifting technique, gym etiquette and safe movement in a friendly introductory class.

Details and requirements

You must complete a teen gym induction before taking part. A parent or legal guardian must attend the induction. Previous lifting experience is not required.

Teen induction and programme details

Teens · 13–17

Teen Fitness Academy

A structured introduction to exercise, warm-ups and gym skills, with progression from beginner to intermediate.

Details and requirements

The Bishopstown Teen Fitness Academy costs €54 a month by direct debit and runs continuously. Academy membership includes teen gym and swimming outside class times, subject to session availability and admission rules. An induction is required before using the gym.

Teen induction and programme details

Wellbeing

MoveWell

An HSE-funded exercise programme for adults with specified health conditions. Contact the team before joining.

Details and requirements

For adults with type 2 diabetes, asthma, COPD or cardiovascular disease. Participants must be able to exercise independently without a mobility aid. Sessions include cardio, strength, balance and stretching, led by a qualified exercise professional. Ask the team to confirm eligibility and how to join.

Ask about MoveWell

Wellbeing

Movin’ Chat

A six-week programme combining gentle movement, balance and coordination with time to socialise.

Details and requirements

Circuit activities take place indoors or outdoors, depending on the weather. Tea or light refreshments follow each class. Contact the team for the next programme dates and availability.

Ask about Movin’ Chat

Arrive 10–15 minutes before your class. At Bishopstown, the studios are reached through the rear of the main gym. Availability and entry requirements vary by programme and centre.

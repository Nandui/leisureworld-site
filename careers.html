Careers at
LW Management

At LW Management, we create spaces where communities can live well, stay active, and feel they belong.

Founded in 1997, we have grown into one of Cork City's leading leisure, recreation, and cultural providers. Today, our team of over 110 dedicated employees welcomes more than 1 million visitors every year across our facilities.

As part of LW Management, we operate:

  • LeisureWorld Bishopstown
  • LeisureWorld Churchfield
  • LeisureWorld Douglas

Together, we provide modern leisure facilities and community spaces designed to promote wellbeing, activity, and inclusion.

01

Community-rooted

Not-for-profit and locally run for over 25 years. Every surplus is reinvested into facilities and community programmes.

02

110+ colleagues

A diverse team spanning fitness, aquatics, administration, facilities, and customer experience across three sites.

03

1 million+ visits/year

Real impact at scale. Every day our team helps Cork people stay active, healthy, and connected to their community.

04

Multi-site flexibility

Work across three modern facilities with room to grow, develop, and move within the organisation over time.

Our Culture

Why Work With Us?

We believe great experiences start with great people. Our teams are passionate about creating welcoming environments where everyone feels included.

We work together with purpose, energy, and a shared commitment to serving our communities. Whether you're starting your career or looking for your next move, LW Management is a place to grow, develop, and make a genuine difference.

We're not a commercial gym chain. We're a community facility with community values and that shapes everything about how we work.

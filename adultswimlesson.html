Adult Swim Lessons

It's never too late to learn — or to get better. Whether you're stepping into the pool for the first time as an adult, refining your stroke, or joining a swim squad, there's a lane and a group here for you.

Two Ways to Swim as an Adult

Structured lessons, or a swim community

Choose between our step-by-step Learn2Swim programme — with dedicated Beginner and Improver levels — or join Otter Swim Club, our friendly weekly squad for adults who already swim confidently.

Programme 1

Adult Learn2Swim

A structured six-week course in small, adult-only groups. Choose the level that matches where you are today — no judgement, no children in the lane, just steady progress with a qualified teacher.

1
Level 1

Beginner

For adults with little or no swimming experience. We start in the shallow end and build water confidence at your pace — no pressure, no deep water until you're ready.

  • Getting comfortable in the water
  • Breathing & face-in-water technique
  • Floating on your front and back
  • Kicking with support (kickboards)
  • First strokes across the pool
  • Basic pool safety
2
Level 2

Improver

For adults who can already swim a length but want to refine technique, learn new strokes, or build the stamina to swim continuously without stopping.

  • Refining freestyle technique & breathing
  • Introduction to breaststroke
  • Backstroke fundamentals
  • Building continuous swim distance
  • Turns & efficient breathing patterns
  • Prep for open-water & club swimming
Duration 6 Weeks
Format Weekly · 45 min
Group Size Max 8
Next Course €84 · 6 weeks
Adult Swim Club
Programme 2

Otter Swim Club

A friendly, coached swim club for adults who already swim confidently and want the structure of a weekly squad session. Think of it as a run club, but in the pool — with tailored sets, technique feedback, and a great community of Cork swimmers to train alongside.

Weekly Sessions Tue & Thu evenings, Sat morning
60 Min Coached sets & drills
Prerequisite Swim 200m continuous
Community Adults only · all abilities welcome

How to enrol

1

Choose your track

Learn2Swim for structured lessons with clear progression, or Otter Swim Club for coached weekly training with a community.

2

Check availability

Contact reception to see current class times, or check the timetable online. New Learn2Swim intakes run every six weeks.

3

Book & get swimming

Enrol online or at reception. Bring togs, a towel and goggles — we'll take care of the rest. First session is the hardest part.

Not sure which one is for you?

Give us a call or drop by any centre — we're happy to talk it through and help you pick the right starting point. There's no wrong answer, and no pressure to commit before you're ready.

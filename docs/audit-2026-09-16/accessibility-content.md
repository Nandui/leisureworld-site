# Physical access content review

Updated `accessibility.html` from the captured operator accessibility and
Functional Zone pages. Replaced a circular “Published access information” link
and generic “ask about facilities” cards with useful parking, changing, pool
entry, hoist, staff-assistance, lift and adaptable gym-equipment information.
Added direct centre phone numbers and an access-specific email route.

The source accessibility page does **not** identify which named centre each
physical feature belongs to. The three official centre pages also do not provide
a complete centre-by-centre access inventory. The new page therefore presents
the published features as available LeisureWorld facilities and explicitly asks
visitors to confirm their chosen centre's arrangements; it does not claim every
feature exists at all three centres. A future operator-maintained access inventory
could improve this detail without guessing now.

The Functional Zone is explicitly located at **Bishopstown**. Its access route
remains **HSE Community Physiotherapy only**, and LeisureWorld does not decide
who is referred. The enquiry link is for the space/equipment, not a booking or
self-referral action. No healthcare recommendation was added.

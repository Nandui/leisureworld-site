Safe Selling Supplements 
& Sport Food Statement 
 
 
At LeisureWorld, the health, safety, and informed choice of our customers are our highest priorities. In accordance with 
our Clean Sport & Anti-Doping Policy and standard-setting bodies like Sport Ireland, Ireland Active, and the Food Safety 
Authority of Ireland (FSAI), we operate a fully compliant and transparent retail environment for sports foods and dietary 
supplements.  
 
This statement applies to all sports nutrition products, sports drinks, and protein bars sold on our premises, including all 
items dispensed via our vending machines. 
 
• Legal Registration & Food Safety Compliance 
o HSE & FSAI Notification: LeisureWorld is fully registered with the local Environmental Health Office 
(EHO) of the Health Service Executive (HSE) as a business selling food supplements and sports food 
products. We have officially notified the Food Safety Authority of Ireland (FSAI) of all relevant products in 
our retail inventory. 
o Traceability & Packaging: All sports foods and food supplements for sale on site are sourced exclusively 
from reputable manufacturers holding ISO 9001 quality standards. They are sold only in their original, 
sealed manufacturer packaging (never decanted or loose). 
o Consumer Rights: In strict adherence to the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC) 
policy and the Sale of Goods and Supply of Services Act, 1980, all customer pricing, product information, 
and statutory rights are clearly displayed, accurate, and kept up to date. 
 
• Marketing and Health Claims 
o No Unauthorised Claims: LeisureWorld strictly complies with Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 on nutrition 
and health claims. 
o We do not promote, display, or tolerate non-authorised health claims. This includes any marketing 
materials or verbal product recommendations from our staff regarding unsubstantiated benefits, 
particularly those relating to rapid weight gain or weight loss. 
 
• Product Screening & Quality Standards (Exceeded & Outstanding) 
o To protect our recreational customers and competitive athletes alike, we screen our product inventory 
against rigorous safety frameworks: 
o The Sport Ireland Checklist: All retail products are verified against the Sport Ireland supplement checklist 
to ensure safety and regulatory compliance. 
o Informed-Sport Batch Testing: Moving beyond standard compliance, we proactively verify our inventory 
against the Informed-Sport List. We focus on sourcing sports foods and supplements that have been 
independently batch-tested to ensure they are free from WADA-prohibited substances, actively 
supporting our members in meeting their personal strict liability obligations.  
 
For more information on our zero-tolerance policy regarding prohibited substances, please consult the LeisureWorld 
Clean Sport & Anti-Doping Policy available on our website or speak to the Duty Manager. Verification tools can be 
accessed directly at www.sportireland.ie/anti-doping.

Clean Sport & Anti-Doping Policy 
 
Commitment to Clean Sport 
LeisureWorld is committed to protecting the health of our customers, the integrity of our community, and the 
spirit of fair play. The facility operates a strict zero-tolerance policy toward the use, possession, or 
distribution of Performance Enhancing Drugs (PEDs) and prohibited substances, applying equally to 
competitive athletes and recreational customers. 
 
Regulatory Alignment 
This policy is aligned with the standards and frameworks established by Sport Ireland, Ireland Active, the 
World Health Organization (WHO), and the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA). 
 
Customer Obligations & Strict Liability 
By using LeisureWorld facilities, you expressly agree to the following conditions: 
 
● Compliance with WADA Code: Customers must not use, possess, traffic, or attempt to distribute any 
substances or methods featured on the current WADA Prohibited List (2026), including but not limited to 
anabolic agents, hormones, and stimulants. 
 
● Strict Liability: In accordance with international anti-doping principles, the rule of "Strict Liability" 
applies. Customers are solely and personally responsible for any substances found within their body, 
equipment, or personal property while on LeisureWorld premises. 
 
● Supplement and Medication Awareness: It is the sole responsibility of the customer to verify that any 
medications or nutritional supplements they consume are safe and permitted.  
○ LeisureWorld strongly advises members to check medications via the Medcheck tool on 
SportIreland.ie (or Eirpharm) and to use only batch-tested supplements (e.g., Informed-
Sport). 
 
Enforcement and Termination of Membership 
● Right to Investigate: LeisureWorld reserves the right to investigate any suspected breaches of this 
policy to ensure the safety and integrity of the facility environment. 
● Immediate Termination: Any customer found to be using, possessing, or distributing prohibited 
substances or PEDs within or around LeisureWorld premises will have their membership/access 
terminated immediately and permanently. 
● Reporting: In cases involving the illicit distribution or trafficking of prohibited substances, LeisureWorld 
reserves the right to report the matter to Sport Ireland and relevant law enforcement authorities. 
 
Confidential Reporting & Resources 
Customers who have questions regarding this policy, or who wish to report a suspected breach in total 
confidence, should contact the Duty Manager. Further information and verification tools can be accessed 
directly at www.sportireland.ie/anti-doping.

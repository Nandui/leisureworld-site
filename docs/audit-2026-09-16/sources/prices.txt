Membership & Prices
At LeisureWorld we are committed to providing quality facilities and services to everyone that wishes to avail of them. As a public health, fitness and leisure facility we have a range of pricing options to ensure that our facilities are accessible to the people of Cork and beyond.
On this page will see a list of prices for each of our sites, please click on the link for each site to view more details on that site. Please note that prices as well as facilities and services offered can and does vary between sites.
Our ethos is Every-Body-Belongs, we try to replicate this through our various pricing options and our welcoming attitude to people of all ages and from all walks of life.

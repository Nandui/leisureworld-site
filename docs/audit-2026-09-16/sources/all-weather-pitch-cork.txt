Astro Turf Pitches
All Weather Pitches Cork
LeisureWorld provides you
flood lit all weather astro turf pitches
in both Bishopstown and Churchfield locations in Cork. Grab your friends or colleagues and make a weekly game part of your physical activity regime.
A game of football with friends is a great way for you to keep fit, socialise and have fun all at the same time. At both LeisureWorld Bishopstown and LeisureWorld Churchfield we provide 5 a side or 6 a side pitches ideal for weekly games of soccer after work or college.
Both of our sites have ample free parking as well as changing and shower facilities for you to use. All of our synthetic astro turf pitches are floodlit.
We would recommend that you block book your slot if you want to reserve the same time each week

Lifeguard Course
THE NPLQ is a UK lifeguard course and qualification that is recognized internationally and by all employers in Ireland.
The NPLQ Course is hosted at one of our
LeisureWorld facilities here in Cork
.

You will be assessed by an independent trainer assessor at the end of the course.
Prerequisites to book:
Candidate must be at least 16 years of age
Candidate must be able to swim 50m in less than 60 sec.
Candidate must be able to swim 100m  continuously
Candidate must be able to surface dive and comfortably thread water
36 hours of training needs to be completed by the candidate before going forward for assessment.
Swimwear, shorts and T-shirts is needed for the duration of the course.
Cost: €500 including all learning materials, assessment, logbook & 2 year qualification.
Renewal candidates also welcome on these courses, 20 hour course.

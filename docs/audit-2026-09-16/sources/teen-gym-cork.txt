Teen Gym Academy
Learn to lift the right way!
Our Teen Fitness Academy offers the perfect way for teenagers to start their Gym Journey the right way! In partnership with our instructors, you will be introduced to exercises in a safe and correct way, ensuring that you are prepared to embrace your fitness journey safely and productively. Starting with level 1 – Beginners which include the basics of exercise, principles of learning environment, safety and gaining confidence on the gym floor. These are all essential while understanding warm-up, cool-downs, and basic body weight exercises during these sessions.
Once ready, you can then move through the level 2 – Intermediates, ensuring that you kick off your next steps towards a well rounded fitness routine that not only produces visible results but more importantly, increases your wellbeing and overall confidence.

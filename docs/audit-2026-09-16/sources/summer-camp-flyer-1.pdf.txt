KIDS CAMPKIDS CAMPINFORMATIONINFORMATION
The camp Drop off time is 9am and collection
is 3pm.
Camp Essentials:
Each day we would ask parents to ensure
children have:
Comfortable shoes / trainers suited for
indoor / outdoor play and sports
Swimwear and a towel, (swim hats are
compulsory in LeisureWorld, goggles are
optional, please also note that we ask that
all kids are able to fully change themselves
for the swimming time)
A packed lunch (Small & Big Lunch)
Bottle of water 
Hat & Sun cream if necessary
Please note there will not be any Vending
machine access throughout the week

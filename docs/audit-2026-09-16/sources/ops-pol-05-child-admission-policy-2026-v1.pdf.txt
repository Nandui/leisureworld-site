Child Admission Policy 
 
 
Purpose 
The purpose of this policy is to ensure the safety, wellbeing, and effective supervision of all children utilising the 
leisure facilities, including the swimming pool, gym, and organised camps. It provides a framework for parents,  
guardians, and staff to maintain a secure environment in compliance with national safety standards. 
 
Scope 
This policy applies to all minors (under 18 years of age), their parents/guardians, and staff members within the 
LeisureWorld facility. It covers general public admissions, swimming lessons, and specialised kids' camps. 
 
Principles 
• Safety First: The safety of the child is the paramount consideration in all 
• activities. 
• Parental Responsibility: Parents or guardians maintain primary responsibility for their children unless the 
child is signed into a programmed activity. 
• Inclusion: Services are provided consistently and transparently, regardless of disability or other protected 
characteristics. 
• Compliance: All users must adhere to LeisureWorld rules and staff instructions to maintain a pleasant 
environment. 
 
Supervision and Access 
LeisureWorld operates strictly enforced supervision ratios to ensure the safety of all minors. These ratios are based 
on national safety guidelines and vary by activity. 
• All specific age-related access rules and supervision ratios are outlined in the Appendix: Access and 
Supervision Requirements 
• Supervision ratios are determined by industry safety standards, risk assessments, and insurance 
requirements. 
• Management reserves the right to amend these ratios based on safety assessments. Parents are 
responsible for reviewing these requirements prior to their visit. 
 
General Requirements 
• Health Declarations: All children participating in activities and gym patrons must have a completed screening 
or health declaration form on file. 
• Medical Conditions: Parents must disclose any medical conditions (e.g., Asthma, Epilepsy, Diabetes) or 
additional needs to ensure staff can provide necessary accommodations. 
• Induction: All teens must complete a gym safety induction prior to using the gym or attending fitness classes. 
• Code of Conduct: LeisureWorld maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding the use or possession of drugs, 
alcohol, or aggressive/anti-social behavior. Any individual found in breach of this code will be refused entry 
or removed from the premises immediately. 
• Exclusions: Management reserves the right to exclude individuals for behavioural issues or non-compliance 
with safety rules. 
 
Review 
This policy will be reviewed annually, or earlier if required

Summer Camps & Groups 
Groups must provide a manifest of all participants and adhere to the following: 
• Dry-Side Activities: 2 : 25 (Leader to Child). 
• Wet-Side Activities: 2 : 25 (In addition to the Lifeguards). 
Safety Briefing: A 5-minute safety briefing must be conducted by the Duty Manager for all camp participants before 
the first pool session.

Hygiene Recovery Times 
To prevent the spread of infection (Cryptosporidium), the following exclusion periods apply: 
• Standard Illness: 48 Hours clear of symptoms. 
• Confirmed Waterborne Infection: 14 Days clear of symptoms. 
• Pre-shower entry: All bathers must shower thoroughly before entering the pool deck

Careers at LeisureWorld
We were founded in 1997 and over 22 years later, we have become one of the leading leisure, recreation and cultural providers in Cork City. Over 110 passionate staff members serve a footfall of over 1 million each year and within our buildings, you’ll find 6 swimming pools, 2 gyms, 12 football pitches, an 18-hole golf course and a church dating back to the 12th century that now serves as a cultural & heritage centre.
LeisureWorld Cork
As part of our portfolio, we run LeisureWorld Bishopstown, Churchfield and Douglas.

About our Organisation
Our teams are dedicated to living well and through our modern facilities, we service the needs of the public and provide excellent local amenities for our communities. We believe that fitness is for everyone and our motto is EveryBody Belongs.
Our team has one major thing in common: we come together to work hard and with a sense of purpose, bringing the best of ourselves to create a wonderful experience and an atmosphere that is friendly, welcoming and enjoyable for everyone.

Centre Manager
Pay:
€42,000.00 per year
Job Description
– Centre Manager
Full time positions required, 39 hours p/w
Location
– LW Group (LeisureWorld Bishopstown & LeisureWorld Churchfield)
(we are looking for two candidates)
LeisureWorld have an exciting opportunity for an experienced Centre Manager to join our team.
Key Responsibilities:
The Centre Manager is responsible for running one LeisureWorld site safely and professionally day to day. You are the main site manager for normal operations. You are responsible for keeping the building safe, organised, clean, properly staffed and ready for customers. You lead Duty Managers, Shift Supervisors, Department Supervisors, and the wider site team, deal with problems early, manage staff performance and make practical decisions within your authority. You report to the Operations Manager and know when a significant safety, people, financial or operational issue needs to be escalated.
Your goal is to make sure your site runs safely, smoothly and consistently. That means:
Customers feel welcome, safe and respected.
Staff know what they are doing and what standard is expected.
The building is clean, organised, staffed and ready for use.
Health and safety procedures are followed.
People and performance issues are dealt with properly.
Incidents and complaints are followed through.
Rosters and staffing match the needs of the site.
Problems are escalated before they become bigger issues.
You will work with:
Operations Manager
Duty Managers
Department Supervisors
Shift Supervisors and managers across the LeisureWorld sites
Reception Team
Lifeguards and Swim Teachers
Gym Team
Cleaning and Support staff
Customers, parents, guardians, contractors and members of the public
You will:
Act as the day-to-day operational lead for your assigned site.
Make sure management cover, opening, operating and closing arrangements are in place.
Check that the building is safe, clean, staffed and ready for customers. Keep an eye on busy areas, queues, pool use, gym use, lessons, clubs and changing rooms.
Set priorities and move staff or resources when the operation needs it.
Make practical site-level decisions within your role and authority.
Follow up problems and make sure agreed actions are completed.
Escalate significant or unresolved issues to the Operations Manager.
Ideal Candidate:
Experience in leisure or retail an advantage.
A proven track record of leading and motivating a team.
A passion for delivering customer experience above and beyond customers’ expectations.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
Enthusiastic & self-motivated.
Dedicated and Hardworking.
Flexibility and availability

Football in Cork

All-weather pitches

Get your group together for a game at Bishopstown or Churchfield.

Choose your pitch

Bishopstown

Four floodlit pitches for six-a-side football, beside the pool and gym. Changing rooms, showers, lockers and free parking are available.

One hour: €50 off peak / €66 peak.

Bishopstown directions and contact details

Churchfield

Eight floodlit five-a-side pitches at Sam Allen Pitches, across the road from the leisure centre. Changing rooms, showers, lockers and free parking are available.

One hour: €45 off peak / €55 peak.

Churchfield directions and contact details

The price for your chosen time is shown during booking. Contact reception if you need help checking a rate.

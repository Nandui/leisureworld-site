Before you join.

What does a membership include?

Bishopstown and Churchfield memberships cover access to those two centres. Classes have a separate add-on. Douglas offers its own pool membership. Specialist programmes, lessons and other activities may have separate fees or entry requirements.

How does the classes add-on work?

The published add-on is €20 per month with direct debit or €200 with a 12-month prepaid membership. It must be selected when you sign up.

Are discounted options available?

Discounts are available for eligible students, older adults and unemployed visitors. Teen memberships have their own entry requirements. Check the evidence and conditions with reception before joining.

Membership terms
Can I freeze or cancel?

Notice periods and conditions apply. Read the membership terms and contact your centre before making changes.

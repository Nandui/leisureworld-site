Children’s activity camps

Games, crafts, sport and pool time for children aged 5–10.

The next camp dates

The summer 2026 camp season has finished. The last published week ended on 21 August 2026. The next dates and prices have not yet been announced.

Camps run at Bishopstown and Churchfield. There are no camps currently advertised for Douglas. Contact your preferred centre to ask about the next programme.

Ask about upcoming camps

What children can expect

Age groups take part in arts and crafts, team games, indoor and outdoor activities, and pool play. Outdoor activities depend on the weather. Children need to be able to change themselves for swimming.

The most recent programme ran Monday–Friday, 9am–3pm, at €130 per child per week. These are summer 2026 details, not a promise of future dates, hours or prices.

What to bring

  • Comfortable clothes and trainers for indoor and outdoor play.
  • Swimwear, a swim hat and a towel; goggles are optional.
  • A packed lunch with food for two breaks and a bottle of water.
  • A hat and sun cream when needed.

Children do not have access to vending machines during camp. Let the team know about medical conditions or additional support needs before attending.

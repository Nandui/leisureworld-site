Water Safety Ireland Level 1 Assistant Swim Teacher

Looking to start your journey in swim teaching? Join the upcoming Water Safety Ireland Level 1 Assistant Swim Teacher Course at LeisureWorld Bishopstown this June.

This nationally recognised course is ideal for anyone interested in developing the skills and confidence needed to assist with swimming lessons in a safe, supportive, and enjoyable environment.

Course Details

  • Location: LeisureWorld Bishopstown
  • Dates: 13th, 14th, 20th & 21st June
  • Time: 9:00am – 6:00pm each day
  • Age Requirement: 16 years and over
  • Cost: €500

Course Overview

  • Communication and teaching skills
  • Techniques for assisting swimmers of all ages and abilities

The course combines practical, pool-based training with essential teaching theory, helping candidates build confidence while gaining valuable hands-on experience.

Whether you are interested in becoming a swim teacher, working with children, or developing lifesaving and teaching skills, this course provides a strong foundation in aquatic education.

Spaces are limited, so early booking is advised.

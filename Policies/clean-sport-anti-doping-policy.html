Clean sport & anti-doping

The centre’s policy on prohibited substances and safe participation.

The centre’s policy

LeisureWorld’s published policy applies to competitive athletes and recreational customers. It sets out a zero-tolerance approach to the use, possession or distribution of performance-enhancing drugs and prohibited substances on the premises.

The document explains customer responsibilities, supplement and medication awareness, investigations, termination of membership and confidential reporting. Read the full published policy for its conditions.

Questions or concerns

Speak to the Duty Manager if you have a question or wish to report a suspected breach in confidence.

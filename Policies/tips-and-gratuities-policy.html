Overview

Tips & Gratuities Policy

LeisureWorld is required by law under the Payment of Wages Act 1991 (as amended) to distribute to our employees any tips or gratuities received. We are committed to our obligations under this legislation and the associated SI 545 of 2022 Payment of Wages Act 1991 (Display of Notices) Regulations 2022.

Distribution

How Tips Are Distributed

Staff in the Café only receive gratuities in the following form:

  • Cash — all tips are distributed on the day. Cash tips and gratuities are received directly by staff from customers and LeisureWorld does not involve itself in this process.
Signed by Mark McManus, CEO — August 2024

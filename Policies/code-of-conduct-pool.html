All Policies
Applies to All pools at Bishopstown, Churchfield, and Gus Healy Pool Douglas.
These rules apply to all pool users across all LeisureWorld centres. All users must obey the instructions of lifeguards at all times.

Pool Rules

01

Obey lifeguards at all times.

02

Swimming hats must be worn at all times.

03

No running in the pool area.

04

No diving.

05

No bombing or jumping into the pool.

06

No horseplay.

07

No petting.

08

No ducking.

09

Children under 8 must be accompanied in the water by an adult at all times.

10

Swim diapers must be worn by toddlers and young children.

11

Proper swimwear must be worn at all times.

12

No outdoor footwear is permitted in the pool area.

13

No food, drink, or glass bottles in the changing area, gallery, or pool area.

14

Adults must remain in the centre with children aged 10 years and under.

15

The Sauna and Steam Room are strictly for over 18s only.

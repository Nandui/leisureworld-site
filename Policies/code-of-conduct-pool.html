Pool Rules

Pool rules checked 16 September 2026. Children’s supervision follows the current admission policy: under 10s need an adult in the water (maximum two children per adult); ages 10–12 need an adult in the building.

These rules apply to all pool users across all LeisureWorld centres. All users must obey the instructions of lifeguards at all times.

Pool Rules

  1. Obey lifeguards at all times.

  2. Swimming hats must be worn at all times.

  3. No running in the pool area.

  4. No diving.

  5. No bombing or jumping into the pool.

  6. No horseplay.

  7. No petting.

  8. No ducking.

  9. Follow the current children’s admission and supervision requirements.

  10. Swim diapers must be worn by toddlers and young children.

  11. Proper swimwear must be worn at all times.

  12. No outdoor footwear is permitted in the pool area.

  13. No food, drink, or glass bottles in the changing area, gallery, or pool area.

  14. The Sauna and Steam Room are restricted to people aged 18 and over.

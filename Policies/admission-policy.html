Admission Policy

This policy is subject to change. The most up-to-date version is available directly on our website.

General Admission

  • Admission to the Leisure Centre is subject to the applicable admission fees, either through membership or pay-as-you-go charges.
  • Members must scan their barcode or check in at reception to record their attendance and gain access to the facilities.
  • Access is based on the current timetable of activities, which is subject to change.
  • Admission to the Sauna and Steam Room is strictly restricted to persons aged 18 years and over.
  • Adult gym sessions are open to those aged 18 years and over.
  • Student gym and swim sessions are open to those aged 16 years and over.
  • Teen gym and swim sessions are available to those aged 13–15 years.
  • All teens must complete a gym safety induction prior to using the gym or attending fitness classes.
  • All gym patrons must complete and sign a Health Declaration upon registration.
  • If you have, or suspect you may have, a medical condition that could interfere with safe exercise, you must seek advice from a relevant medical professional and follow that guidance before using any facility.
  • Pool use is subject to maximum bather loads for safety reasons. If capacity is reached, admission may be temporarily restricted.
  • Entry will be refused to anyone under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • During swimming lessons, a parent/guardian is required to remain on site for the duration of the lesson.

Children's Admission

  • Children under 10 years must be accompanied in the water by a responsible adult (18+), at a ratio of 1 adult to a maximum of 2 children. Supervision from the viewing gallery or poolside is not considered adequate supervision.
  • Children aged 10–12 years must have a responsible adult (18+) present within the building.
  • Children must be supervised by a responsible adult at all times while in the centre.
  • Supervising adults must adhere to the required safe supervision ratios while children are in the pool.

Supervision age bands confirmed by LeisureWorld on 16 September 2026. See the child admission policy for lessons, camps and health requirements.

Conduct & Compliance

  • All users must comply with the rules and guidelines displayed throughout the centre.
  • These rules are in place to ensure that all visitors can enjoy a safe, welcoming, and pleasant environment.
  • Any person who does not comply with this policy or who disregards the instructions of Management may be refused entry or asked to leave the premises.

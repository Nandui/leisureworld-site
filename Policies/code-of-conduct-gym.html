Gym & Fitness Class Rules

Read the rules for using gym equipment and attending fitness classes.

These rules apply to all gym users and fitness class attendees across all LeisureWorld centres. Failure to comply may result in removal from the facility.

Gym Rules

  1. Persons under the age of 18 are not permitted admission to the adult gym, with the exception of Teen Gym sessions or group bookings and classes.

  2. No mobile phones or photography are permitted in the gym.

  3. No food or chewing gum is permitted in the gym.

  4. Always return weights and all other equipment to the proper storage area after use.

  5. Clips and collars must always be applied when using weight bars.

  6. All users must comply with the instructions of staff at all times.

  7. Appropriate clothing and footwear must be worn at all times in the gym and studio.

  8. Treat all equipment with respect.

Fitness Class Rules

  1. Class attendees must work within their own physical limits and must not put themselves or others at risk of injury.

  2. Class attendees who do not follow the studio rules may be asked to leave by a member of staff.

  3. All other class attendees must be considered at all times. No shouting, loud swearing, or other inappropriate behaviour deemed disruptive by the class instructor. Be polite and friendly.

  4. All class attendees must bring a towel and a drink with them to every class.

  5. All class attendees are expected to wipe down their own equipment after each class.

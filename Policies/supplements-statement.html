Supplements & sports food

How LeisureWorld’s published retail policy covers supplements and sports food.

What this statement covers

The statement applies to sports nutrition products, sports drinks and protein bars sold on site, including vending machines. It covers food safety and registration, traceability and sealed packaging, product information, marketing claims, and product screening.

Use sports nutrition products responsibly, following the manufacturer’s instructions and relevant professional advice. Speak to the Duty Manager if you have a question about a product sold in the centre.

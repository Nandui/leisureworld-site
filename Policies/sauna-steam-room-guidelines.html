Sauna & Steam Room Guidelines

Read entry restrictions, safety advice and guidance for using the sauna and steam room.

You Should Not Use the Sauna or Steam Room If:Rules for UseUsage Guidelines

Read this page for accessible information, or open the complete published document (PDF, 1 page). Source checked 16 September 2026.

Please read all health risk information carefully before using the sauna or steam room. If you have any concerns, speak to a member of staff before entering.

You Should Not Use the Sauna or Steam Room If:

  1. You are pregnant.

  2. You have recently exercised.

  3. You are dehydrated.

  4. You have consumed a meal within the last one and a half hours.

  5. You have consumed alcohol.

  6. You have any infectious skin disease, sores, or wounds.

  7. You suffer from circulatory problems.

  8. You suffer from low or high blood pressure.

  9. You suffer from heart disease.

  10. You suffer from kidney disease.

  11. You suffer from an inability to perspire.

  12. You suffer from cardiovascular or respiratory conditions.

  13. You suffer from epilepsy.

  14. You suffer from diabetes.

  15. You suffer from fainting or dizziness.

Rules for Use

  1. Patrons must be 18 years or over.

  2. Appropriate swimwear must be worn at all times.

  3. Patrons must shower before and after use.

  4. Patrons must not use the sauna or steam room for more than 15 minutes at a time.

  5. Patrons are asked not to interfere with the thermostat.

  6. Patrons are asked to drink water regularly during use.

  7. Glass bottles, razors, or any sharp items are not permitted.

  8. Consumption of alcohol or food is not permitted.

Usage Guidelines

  1. It is advisable to remove contact lenses before entering.

  2. It is advisable to remove any metal items as these may become hot.

  3. It is advisable to sit down upon entering.

  4. It is advisable to relax for 5–10 minutes to allow the body temperature to raise and the perspiration to cleanse your pores.

  5. It is advisable to take a cold shower when you leave.

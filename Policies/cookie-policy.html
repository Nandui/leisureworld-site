Your privacy

Cookie policy

A simple explanation of cookies on this website.

Cookies on these pages

This website does not set first-party cookies or use browser storage for advertising, analytics or preferences. Its menus, activity filters and page navigation work without saving a personal profile.

Fonts and images are served with the website. Social media, maps, app stores and booking services are links: they are not embedded trackers on these pages.

When you open another service

The LeisureWorld app, Legend booking service, gift voucher store, job application service and other external websites may use cookies or similar storage for sign-in, checkout, security and other functions. Read the cookie and privacy information shown by the service you are using.

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies on an external booking service may prevent sign-in or payment from working.

Questions and updates

If the website’s use of cookies changes, this page will be updated and any required choice will be provided before optional cookies are used.

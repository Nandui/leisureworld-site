Child admission policy

The supervision and safety arrangements for children visiting LeisureWorld.

Public swimming supervision

These age and supervision requirements apply during general public swimming sessions.

  • Children under 3: must be accompanied in the water at all times by an adult aged 18 or over, with a maximum of one child per adult.
  • Children aged 3–7: must be accompanied in the water at all times by an adult aged 18 or over, with a maximum of two children per adult.
  • Children aged 8–12: may swim without an adult in the water. An adult aged 18 or over must remain within the leisure centre for the whole visit and keep the child in direct view from poolside.
  • Aged 13 and over: may swim independently, subject to their swimming ability and the pool rules.

Important safety information

  • Children who are weak swimmers or non-swimmers must be accompanied by an adult in the water, whatever their age.
  • Children must stay within areas that suit their swimming ability.
  • Armbands and flotation aids do not replace the need for appropriate adult supervision.
  • Adults supervising children in the water must stay close enough to step in immediately, and must not leave children unattended where supervision is required.
  • Parents and guardians remain responsible for the safety and supervision of children in their care.
  • Our lifeguards may intervene, or restrict access to areas or activities, where they have concerns about a child’s safety or swimming ability. The centre may set stricter supervision requirements where individual circumstances or safety concerns call for them.
  • Admission may be refused where the required supervision is not in place, and children and accompanying adults may be asked to leave the pool if safety requirements are not followed.

These requirements follow recognised Swim Ireland swimming pool safety guidance. Separate arrangements may apply to swimming lessons, clubs and organised activities.

Lessons, camps and teen gym

A parent or guardian must remain on site throughout swimming lessons. Parents and guardians keep primary responsibility for children unless they are signed into a programmed activity.

Lesson supervision depends on ability and water depth. The published ratios are one teacher to eight children at levels 1–2, ten at level 3, and twelve at levels 4–6.

Camps and groups must provide a participant list. The published staffing ratio is two leaders to 25 children for dry activities and pool activities, in addition to lifeguards for pool activities. Camp participants receive a safety briefing before their first pool session.

Teens must complete a free gym safety induction before using the gym or attending teen classes. Other fitness classes are for those aged 18 and over. Teen sessions are for ages 13–17. Read the teen gym introduction before booking.

Health and preparation

  • Children participating in activities need a completed screening or health declaration form.
  • Parents should tell the team about relevant medical conditions or additional support needs.
  • Everyone must shower before entering the pool deck.
  • The child admission policy requires 48 hours clear of symptoms after a standard illness, and 14 days clear of symptoms after a confirmed waterborne infection. Ask your centre if you are unsure when it is appropriate to return.
  • Drugs, alcohol and aggressive or antisocial behaviour are not permitted. Management may refuse entry or remove people who do not follow safety rules.

This policy applies to minors, parents, guardians and staff. It is reviewed annually, or following any significant incident, change in facilities or update to relevant industry guidance.

Planning a visit

Tell us about any support needs before visiting so the team can help you plan. For other entry conditions, read the general admission policy.

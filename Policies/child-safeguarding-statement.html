Our Statement

Child Safeguarding Statement

"We at LeisureWorld want to make sure that our children are protected and kept safe from harm while they are in the care of the staff and volunteers in LeisureWorld."

We do this by:

  • Giving parents, children and employees information about what we do and what is expected from us.
  • Making sure that our staff and volunteers are carefully selected, Garda Vetted, trained and supervised.
  • Letting parents and children know how to voice their concerns or make a complaint if there is anything they are not happy about.

Designated Liaison Persons

Designated Liaison Persons

At LeisureWorld we provide Designated Liaison Persons (DLPs) at each centre. The primary role of the DLP is to be available to any child or parent who has concerns under the Code of Ethics and under the Swim Ireland Child Protection Complaints Law.

On receipt of a complaint, the DLP meets with their counterparts to assess concerns with reference to the Code of Ethics and Code of Behaviour between workers and children.

The DLP may seek advice from Swim Ireland protection officers and if necessary from either the local Social Service or organisations such as the ISPCC.

The DLP and LeisureWorld have no role in investigating child abuse complaints, but the CEO must be informed of the involvement of statutory authorities in strict confidence.

The Designated Liaison Persons for LeisureWorld Churchfield are Fernando Serina, Justin Theunissen & Jason Sheahan.
